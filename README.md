Shareproof

Make photos, videos, screenshots and PDFs safe to share, on your own computer. For macOS and Windows (Intel/AMD x64 and ARM64 / Snapdragon), plus a free website.

Clean scans photos and videos for personal metadata, Content Credentials and AI markers, then removes all of it without lowering quality. Originals are never changed; each cleaned copy is saved as SHOT_<name> next to the original or in a folder you choose.
Redact reads the text in screenshots, photos of documents and PDFs, finds emails, phone numbers, UPI IDs, Aadhaar, PAN, card and bank details, API keys and dates of birth, and covers them with solid boxes. Copies are saved as REDACTED_<name>.

The web tool is free. The desktop app has a free tier, and Shareproof Pro (a one-time purchase) adds videos, unlimited batches and verification reports.

Built for speed
Shareproof runs one worker per CPU thread, so a folder of thousands of photos is done in seconds to minutes. Videos are copied stream-for-stream — no re-encoding, no quality loss.

Requirements
macOS on Apple Silicon or Intel · Windows 10 or 11 on x64 or ARM (Snapdragon). No internet connection needed to clean files.
